# Surface brief — Inbox

## Scope

Primary app surface: the signed-out disposable inbox at `/`, plus its empty, waiting, reading, expired, and mobile states. The admin domain console inherits the same world at lower volume. Mode: Operate.

## Audience and job

Privacy-conscious visitors who need an address now and a verification mail seconds later, often on a phone while another tab waits. They must be able to get an address, recognize arrival, read safely, copy a code, and discard the box without learning a new tool.

## Chosen direction

Locker Bank: the 24-hour parcel locker as the product's operating metaphor. The address is a claim ticket; messages are parcels; an arrival lights the row. Standard mail-client layout and controls, with the world carried only by type, palette, density, and the arrival LED/scanline signature.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A disposable inbox that behaves like a 24-hour parcel locker — address as claim ticket, messages as parcels, arrival as a lit cell — refusing the soft card-grid SaaS mail client.

OWN-WORLD: Powder-coated locker blue and near-black rail against cool concrete ground; parcel-yellow LED, vermilion alert, verified green; Archivo for working text, Martian Mono for codes, counts, and labels; 1px steel rules, 2–6px corners, barcode and QR furniture, tabular figures, no decorative cards.

STORY: The visitor gets a working address in one action, copies it, waits, sees the LED wake when mail lands, reads the parcel safely, lifts the code, then empties the locker.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A utility bar across the top; left rail holds the active address as a perforated parcel tag with QR and countdown, plus saved lockers below; the center column is the searchable message ledger; the right pane is the opened parcel. Primary action is copy address, with new-address beside it. The signature move is the arrival LED on unread rows, followed by one scanline pass, never a bouncing card.
